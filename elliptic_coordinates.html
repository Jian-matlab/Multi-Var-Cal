Elliptic coordinates (u, v)

x = √((u−1)(1−v)/2), y = √((u+1)(v+1)/2). Curves u = const are confocal ellipses, curves v = const are confocal hyperbolas, and they cross at right angles. Move the sliders to move the point.

Elliptic coordinate system: the (u,v) rectangle is mapped to the (x,y) plane

The formulas give x, y ≥ 0 (first quadrant, strong lines); the faint copies are the mirror images in the other quadrants. Each arrow is tangent to the coordinate line it lies along: ∂r/∂u runs along the v = const hyperbola, ∂r/∂v along the u = const ellipse.
