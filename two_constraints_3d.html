Two constraints in 3D: when are ∇f, ∇g₁, ∇g₂ linearly dependent?

Minimise the portfolio risk f(x, y, z) = x² + 4y² + 9z² subject to g₁ = x + y + z − 1 = 0 (fully invested) and g₂ = 3x + 7y + 11z − 6 = 0 (target return 6%). The stationary points of f on the constraint set are exactly where the three gradients are linearly dependent.
